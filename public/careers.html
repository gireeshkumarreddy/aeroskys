Position 03 · Operational

HR & Recruitment Specialist

Build and manage the sales-talent pipeline behind Aeroskys — TASA recruitment and TAG Manager hiring. Suited to someone who enjoys high-volume recruitment, candidate engagement and KPI-based HR operations.

  • Full-time
  • High-volume recruitment
  • KPI-based HR operations
Apply for this role View role details

Join a team that moves the world forward.

Great people build great opportunities.

Compensation

₹15,000 / month

Fixed gross salary, plus a quarterly performance bonus.

Bonus eligibility is based on measurable recruitment outcomes: qualified hires, joining conversion and retention. Typical realised total: ~₹20,000 a month on average.

Key responsibilities

  • Source and screen candidates for TASA and TAG Manager roles through job portals, social media, referrals and recruitment networks.
  • Explain role structures, commission models and KPIs clearly to applicants.
  • Maintain a daily and weekly recruitment pipeline, tracking candidates from first contact to onboarding.
  • Coordinate interviews, assessments, offer processes and joining formalities.
  • Maintain accurate recruitment records, trackers and HR documentation.
  • Support onboarding, induction and initial KPI communication for new hires.
  • Track early-stage performance at 30, 60 and 90 days in coordination with management.
  • Assist with recruitment marketing, job posts and careers-page content.

Performance expectations

Recruitment is treated as a measurable business function. You will propose your own lean KPI and over-target KPI after joining, and manage:

  • Qualified candidates
  • Screening completions
  • Interviews completed
  • Offers issued & accepted
  • Joining conversion
  • 30- & 90-day retention
  • Source effectiveness

Required skills

  • Communication & screening
  • Candidate sourcing
  • Interview coordination
  • Recruitment administration
  • Excel / Google Sheets
  • Follow-up & organisation
  • Independent, KPI-driven

Preferred experience

Recruitment, HR operations, talent acquisition, sales hiring, BPO recruitment or travel-industry recruitment.

Your first three months

  1. M1Build the channelsOpen sourcing channels, create the candidate database, establish baseline ratios.
  2. M2Scale the pipelineGrow volume, improve conversions and strengthen referral sources.
  3. M3Optimise & hit KPITighten the recruitment system and achieve the agreed KPIs.
